## 🎯 What I'm Focused On
I'm doing my MSc in Computer Science and Engineering at DTU with a cybersecurity specialization. My focus is understanding how software fails, and how to build and run systems that hold up.

---

## 🔐 Cybersecurity
Capture the Flag competitions, reverse engineering and web security. I use tools like Wireshark, Nmap, Ghidra, IDA Free and Hydra, and I took part in the 2026 Danish regional CTF.

## 🏠 Linux & Homelab
Arch Linux as my daily driver, virtual machines with KVM/QEMU, and a Debian homelab where I experiment with self-hosting and Docker.

---

## 🛠️ What I'm Learning
- **Web Application Security** - Finding and understanding common vulnerabilities
- **Reverse Engineering** - Reading and analysing compiled programs
- **Cryptography and Exploitation** - Through CTF challenges
- **Networking and Distributed Systems** - How systems communicate, and where they break
- **Self-hosting** - Containers, services and remote management in a homelab
